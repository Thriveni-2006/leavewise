# Pahunch validation report

Test day (never seen by the model): **2026-10-13** (weekday). The extra safety buffer was learned on **2026-10-12**, a different day. Model trained on 9 days: 2026-10-05, 2026-10-06, 2026-10-07, 2026-10-08, 2026-10-09, 2026-10-10, 2026-10-11, 2026-10-12, 2026-10-14.
Test cases: 16493 rider arrivals (random moments inside hours [8, 13, 18, 22]) over 25 routes.

## 2. Is the plan-for time really enough? (wait + ride; target 90%)

Before the buffer: **87%**. After adding the buffer (what the app uses): **93%**. Typical time alone: 48%.
Ride part alone, P90 covered: 85%. Wait part alone, P90 covered: 88%.

Extra buffer learned (minutes, capped at 20): high +8.6, medium +20.0, low +16.1

## 5. Where it is weakest

- Cases that fell to a fallback level (thin data): 0%.
- One test day is a small test. Re-run with other days (`--holdout`) and report the range.
- Wait time depends on how many bus passes were seen; gaps in GPS make some waits look longer.
- Journeys with transfers and signal-level delay are not validated here. The live check is a replay of a past day (the real feed was not available to us).
