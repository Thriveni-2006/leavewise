# Pahunch validation report

Test day (never seen by the model): **2026-10-12** (weekday). The extra safety buffer was learned on **2026-10-09**, a different day. Model trained on 9 days: 2026-10-05, 2026-10-06, 2026-10-07, 2026-10-08, 2026-10-09, 2026-10-10, 2026-10-11, 2026-10-13, 2026-10-14.
Test cases: 15409 rider arrivals (random moments inside hours [8, 13, 18, 22]) over 25 routes.

## 1. How good is the typical in-vehicle time?

| Method | MAE (min) | MAPE (%) |
|---|---|---|
| Pahunch (hour + day-type median) | 3.67 | 18.5 |
| All-day average (no time-of-day) | 5.04 | 27.4 |
| Published timetable | 6.30 | 31.2 |

## 2. Is the plan-for time really enough? (wait + ride; target 90%)

Before the buffer: **78%**. After adding the buffer (what the app uses): **80%**. Typical time alone: 36%.
Ride part alone, P90 covered: 84%. Wait part alone, P90 covered: 79%.

Extra buffer learned (minutes, capped at 20): high +1.3, medium +0.8, low +15.7

Cost of caution: on average you arrive **17 min** earlier than needed (median 17 min).

## 5. Where it is weakest

- Cases that fell to a fallback level (thin data): 0%.
- One test day is a small test. Re-run with other days (`--holdout`) and report the range.
- Wait time depends on how many bus passes were seen; gaps in GPS make some waits look longer.
- Journeys with transfers and signal-level delay are not validated here. The live check is a replay of a past day (the real feed was not available to us).
