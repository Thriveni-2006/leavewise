# LeaveWise validation report

Test day (never seen by the model): **2026-10-12** (weekday). The extra safety buffer was learned on **2026-10-09**, a different day. Model trained on 9 days: 2026-10-05, 2026-10-06, 2026-10-07, 2026-10-08, 2026-10-09, 2026-10-10, 2026-10-11, 2026-10-13, 2026-10-14.
Test cases: 4009 rider arrivals (random moments inside hours [8, 13, 18, 22]) over 6 routes.

## 2. Is the plan-for time really enough? (wait + ride; target 90%)

Before the buffer: **74%**. After adding the buffer (what the app uses): **83%**. Typical time alone: 32%.
Ride part alone, P90 covered: 83%. Wait part alone, P90 covered: 75%.

Extra buffer learned (minutes, capped at 20): high +3.2, medium +19.0, low +20.0

Cost of caution: on average you arrive **24 min** earlier than needed (median 21 min).

## 4. Where it is weakest

- Cases that fell to a fallback level (thin data): 0%.
- One test day is a small test. Re-run with other days (`--holdout`) and report the range.
- Wait time depends on how many bus passes were seen; gaps in GPS make some waits look longer.
- Journeys with transfers, signal-level delay, and live updates are not validated here.
